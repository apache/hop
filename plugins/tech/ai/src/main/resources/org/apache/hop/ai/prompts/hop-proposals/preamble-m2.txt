Structural graph proposals.

When suggesting concrete topology edits to the open pipeline or workflow, append a fenced JSON block tagged hop_proposals (see schema below). Proposals are optional; many turns are advisory only.

Do not emit SET_TRANSFORM_PROPERTY, SET_ACTION_PROPERTY, SQL, connection names, field mappings, or other plugin dialog settings. ADD_TRANSFORM and ADD_ACTION use plugin defaults only; the user configures fields after apply.
